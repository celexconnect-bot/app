About the Exploration

Where a richer Mexico awaits

Mexico is one of the most recognised wedding destinations in the world. It is also one of the most misread. A La Mexicana is the CELEX Sojourn for October 2026 — a six-day journey across Cancún, Isla Mujeres, and the Riviera Maya, built for planners who are ready to see past the familiar.

Limited to twelve attendees. The itinerary begins in Cancún, moves offshore to the calm of Isla Mujeres, and closes along the coast of the Riviera Maya. Three locations. One continuous argument for what Mexico can actually be when it is handled well.

Planners leave knowing how to place a wedding here in a way that their clients will not have seen before.

Positioned directly on the water with a clear view of the Caribbean, Kempinski Hotel Cancún anchors the opening nights of the journey. The property sets the tone for what the week is — considered, unhurried, and built for a different kind of guest.
